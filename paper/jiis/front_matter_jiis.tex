\title[Reliability Boundaries of HyDE-Style Expansion]{When Does HyDE-Style Query Expansion Help? Reliability Boundaries in Multi-Hop Intelligent Information Retrieval}

\author*[1]{\fnm{Shiyong} \sur{Xiong}}\email{1771856981@qq.com}
\author[1]{\fnm{Xingyun} \sur{Chen}}

\affil[1]{\orgdiv{School of Computer Science and Technology}, \orgname{Chongqing University of Posts and Telecommunications}, \orgaddress{\city{Chongqing}, \postcode{400065}, \country{China}}}

\abstract{Generative query expansion can improve retrieval without establishing reliable multi-hop answering. We examine changes in generator, retriever, corpus, evidence interface and reader, using complete-support retrieval, actual evidence retention and paired answer scores with family-specific Holm correction. A frozen hosted HotpotQA artifact raises all-support hit@10 from 0.630 to 0.868 under Hypothetical Document Embeddings (HyDE), but 292 of 500 generated passages contain exact answers and two pinned 7B generators do not reproduce the hosted direction under a shared dense encoder. In a new random 300-question matrix on 5.23 million paragraphs, a cross-encoder reranker reaches 0.537 complete-support retrieval versus BM25 at 0.350. It raises exact-match accuracy from 0.180 to 0.270 for Qwen and 0.173 to 0.243 for Mistral. HyDE improves retrieval, while its answer gain over BM25 remains unresolved for Mistral. Cross-encoder retrieval gains on the 2WikiMultihopQA split-union corpus also do not establish answer gains. In MultiHop-RAG news documents, standard HyDE underperforms matched question-only dense retrieval and all eight reader/length exact-match comparisons. A fresh 100-question character-budget intervention raises complete support retention from 46\% to 55\% without establishing answer gains. These findings support matched references, actual-input checks and direct reader evaluation as configuration-specific validation steps. Missing external iteration, incomplete costs and absent independent human validation limit transport and deployment claims.}
\keywords{intelligent information retrieval; retrieval-augmented generation; multi-hop question answering; query expansion; reproducibility}
